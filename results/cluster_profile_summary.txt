CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION — BUSINESS PROFILE
=========================================

Model:
K-Means clustering with K=5.

The clusters were interpreted using customer age, annual income,
and spending score.

CLUSTER 0 — LOW INCOME / LOW SPENDING
--------------------------------------
Customer Count: 81
Average Age: 42.72
Average Income: 55.30 k$
Average Spending Score: 49.52

Business Profile:
This is the largest customer segment. Customers have relatively
lower average income and lower spending compared with the
higher-income/high-spending segment.

Potential Business Approach:
Use value-oriented products, regular promotions, and retention
campaigns to maintain engagement.


CLUSTER 1 — HIGH INCOME / HIGH SPENDING
----------------------------------------
Customer Count: 39
Average Age: 32.69
Average Income: 86.54 k$
Average Spending Score: 82.13

Business Profile:
These customers have high average income and high spending scores.
They represent a customer segment with strong purchasing activity.

Potential Business Approach:
Consider premium products, personalized offers, loyalty programs,
and targeted campaigns.


CLUSTER 2 — LOW INCOME / HIGH SPENDING
---------------------------------------
Customer Count: 22
Average Age: 25.27
Average Income: 25.73 k$
Average Spending Score: 79.36

Business Profile:
These customers have relatively low income but high spending scores.
They show strong engagement despite lower average income.

Potential Business Approach:
Use affordable premium-looking products, targeted promotions,
bundles, and loyalty incentives.


CLUSTER 3 — HIGH INCOME / LOW SPENDING
---------------------------------------
Customer Count: 35
Average Age: 41.11
Average Income: 88.20 k$
Average Spending Score: 17.11

Business Profile:
These customers have high average income but a low spending score.
They may represent customers with purchasing capacity but relatively
low engagement with the available products.

Potential Business Approach:
Use personalized recommendations, targeted campaigns, and
customer-engagement offers.


CLUSTER 4 — LOW INCOME / LOW SPENDING
--------------------------------------
Customer Count: 23
Average Age: 45.22
Average Income: 26.30 k$
Average Spending Score: 20.91

Business Profile:
These customers have low average income and low spending scores.
They form a smaller low-income, low-spending segment distinct from
Cluster 0.

Potential Business Approach:
Focus on value-oriented products, discounts, and retention
strategies appropriate for price-sensitive customers.


ACTIONABLE BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
===================================

1. Premium Customer Strategy:
   Target Cluster 1 with premium products, personalized
   recommendations, loyalty benefits, and exclusive campaigns.

2. Engagement Strategy:
   Target Cluster 3 with personalized promotions and
   recommendation-based campaigns to encourage higher spending.

3. Value and Retention Strategy:
   Target Clusters 0, 2, and 4 with appropriate value-oriented
   offers, bundles, discounts, and loyalty incentives based on
   their spending behavior.
